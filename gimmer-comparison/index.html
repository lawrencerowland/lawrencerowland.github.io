The fixed brief and model assumptions

A fictional 7 m² reversible, lichen-grey refuge, rated for 160 km/h wind and −15 °C, with a £465k envelope and a ten-month target. These are scenario requirements, not assessed engineering specifications. Two modules, temporary rig, access windows, action durations, costs and probabilities are explicit toy additions.

Each approach includes only the features needed for its question. Logical steps, abstract time units and calendar windows are not interchangeable; none is a verified ten-month delivery forecast. Permit evidence here is a supplied assumption. Budget is calculated in the sharing/co-design examples; physical load, weather and structural safety are not certified by any tab.
