John Buchan's obscure early novel about someone being chased across the southern uplands after being falsely accused of being a traitor, in the years leading up to the Glorious Revolution.

A year ago I asked AI to map the hero's journey and it only managed a chapter or two. This morning I asked AI to read the book, identify all the journeys he makes, and mark them on a map.

Its done an excellent job and you can interact with the map here. https://lnkd.in/eWkDcqd9 if you click on the red dots, it tells you what is happening to the hero at the time. It took about an hour.

Many of John Buchan's books are free to download here, including this one.
