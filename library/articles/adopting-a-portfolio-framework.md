**Earlier method · original note, 16 May 2020 · reading guidance revised 29 September 2026**

This is the first of the [three approaches in the portfolio-frameworks guide]({{ '/Portfolio-frameworks.html#use-cases' | relative_url }}): start with a modular framework and select the practices that answer a management need.

<span id="markdown-toc-purpose"></span>

## Purpose

The map connects **trigger events → business needs → portfolio responses → service areas**. Read it from left to right as a way of discussing why a practice is needed. It is not a schedule, an executable process or evidence that the listed services were delivered.

[![An earlier map connects trigger events to business needs, proposed portfolio responses and service areas](/images/2020-05-16-Adopt-existing-portfolio-framework/Portfolio-services-mapped-to-business-needs-Rowland-and-Lewis.png)](/images/2020-05-16-Adopt-existing-portfolio-framework/Portfolio-services-mapped-to-business-needs-Rowland-and-Lewis.png)

[Open the original map at full size](/images/2020-05-16-Adopt-existing-portfolio-framework/Portfolio-services-mapped-to-business-needs-Rowland-and-Lewis.png). The diagram is retained unchanged.

For one small reading, find **“Which programmes should I stop, start or continue?”** in the business-needs column. Follow its arrow to **“Choose programmes”** in the portfolio-solutions column. That makes the proposed response visible; it does not yet specify the selection criteria, decision authority or evidence required.

<span id="markdown-toc-benefits"></span>

## Benefits

The original proposition was practical and modular: a framework assembled from experience across several portfolios could provide a starting point, with only the relevant modules adopted. The map helps expose the connection between a business need and a proposed practice. It does not establish that a particular selection is sufficient or effective.

## What remains available

The map and this explanation remain public. The [linked repository](https://github.com/lawrencerowland/Data-Model-for-Project-Frameworks) deliberately retains only the earlier folder structure and its explanation. Its former detailed framework, notebooks and supporting corpus are no longer in the current public copy.
