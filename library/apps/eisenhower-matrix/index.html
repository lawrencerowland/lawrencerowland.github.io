    Decide what needs your attention

    Use urgency for how soon something needs attention and importance for its contribution to the goals you care about. The FranklinCovey description of the four quadrants explains this distinction and the value of protecting important work that is not yet urgent.

    • Urgent and important → Do immediately. Confirm the deadline and identify the next useful action.
    • Important, less urgent → Schedule (Decide). Reserve time before it becomes a crisis.
    • Urgent, less important → Delegate. Consider whether someone else is better placed to handle it and agree ownership.
    • Less urgent and less important → Delete / Defer. Decide whether to drop it or revisit it later.

    Try: prevention versus pressure

    Add the examples. A release blocker at urgency 5 and importance 5 falls under Do immediately. Planning recovery drills at urgency 2 and importance 5 falls under Schedule. Edit its urgency from 2 to 3: it moves across the boundary without becoming any more valuable.

    What this map leaves out

    The 3-point cut-off is a convention of this example. It turns gradual ratings into four broad suggestions, so near-boundary tasks need judgement. The map does not consider task duration, dependencies, capacity, who can accept delegated work, or why a deadline exists. “Delete / Defer” is a suggestion to consider, not an automatic deletion.
