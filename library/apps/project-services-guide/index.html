Start with a real decision

A programme director wants a better weekly risk review. A useful starting offer might produce a source-linked risk summary. Its test would check missed changes, unsupported statements and the time needed to review it against the current process.

These are authored design options and trial questions, not a list of services currently delivered or evidence of performance. Any target, tool choice and delivery commitment still needs agreement.

Keep three things connected

1. Offer

Who needs help, with which decision? Name an owner, a bounded task and the current way of doing it.

2. Artefact

What could that person inspect or use? Specify its inputs, approval point and the assumptions it must reveal.

3. Measure

Agree a unit, denominator, starting value, comparison, observation period and reviewer. Include error and review effort.

Measures below are proposed definitions, not measured results or recommended thresholds. A faster output is useful only if its quality and consequences remain acceptable.

Service options

Choose the kind of help worth discussing. Facilitated and analytical work can be combined with a bounded automation trial; the labels do not rank their value.

Strategic alignment workshop

Facilitated & analytical
Offer to discuss
Make competing objectives and trade-offs explicit with the people who own them.
Artefact to inspect
Strategy map, objective hierarchy and an alignment scorecard with recorded disagreements.
Measure to agree
Traceability: count agreed objectives with an owner and a link to an agreed strategic goal, divided by all objectives in scope. Report unresolved disagreements separately.
Scope and limits
One decision or programme, named decision-makers and a dated strategy. Agreement in a workshop is not evidence that benefits have been delivered.

Project chartering

Facilitated & analytical
Offer to discuss
Agree a starting mandate, boundaries and decision rights before detailed delivery planning.
Artefact to inspect
Charter, responsibility matrix (RACI), governance model and an assumptions log.
Measure to agree
Unresolved mandate questions at approval, and subsequent changes attributed to an ambiguous starting scope. Compare against an agreed review checklist, not a universal clarity score.
Scope and limits
One project and its sponsor. Record scope exclusions, budget basis and who may approve a change; a charter is a baseline for discussion, not a guarantee.

PMO establishment and improvement

Facilitated & analytical
Offer to discuss
Design a project-management office around decisions it must support and services it can sustain.
Artefact to inspect
PMO handbook, process playbooks, measures and a staged improvement roadmap.
Measure to agree
Cadence adherence: completed, useful decision reviews divided by planned reviews in an agreed period. Pair the count with a sample of decisions and user feedback.
Scope and limits
A named office, service boundary and staffing assumption. Use the separate capability worksheet to examine current and future capabilities; this option provides no maturity rating.

Integrated master scheduling

Facilitated & analytical
Offer to discuss
Join dependencies, calendars and resource assumptions into a reviewable delivery plan.
Artefact to inspect
Integrated schedule, what-if scenarios, resource view and float map.
Measure to agree
Track milestone forecast error in days at a fixed forecast horizon; record over-allocation hours by resource and period. Check forecast quality as well as utilisation.
Scope and limits
Defined work packages, reporting cut-off and resource calendars. Record missing links and constraints; a critical path calculation cannot validate its input assumptions.

Risk and issue management

Facilitated & analytical
Offer to discuss
Make uncertainty, existing problems and response ownership visible to decision-makers.
Artefact to inspect
Risk and issue registers, mitigation backlog, assumptions and optional simulation outputs.
Measure to agree
Response follow-through: agreed actions completed by their due dates divided by actions due. If using expected monetary value, state each probability, cost basis and dependency assumption.
Scope and limits
A decision horizon and a consistent risk scale. Keep realised issues separate from uncertain events; changes in a score are not automatically risk reduction.

Change and communications planning

Facilitated & analytical
Offer to discuss
Work with affected people to define what must change in everyday work and what support is needed.
Artefact to inspect
Stakeholder map, readiness questions, communications calendar and feedback record.
Measure to agree
Adoption: people completing an agreed new task successfully divided by people asked to try it. Record support effort and who was not represented in the sample.
Scope and limits
One change, affected groups and a review window. Ask about experience directly; attendance, message reach and expressed enthusiasm do not establish adoption.

Benefits realisation tracking

Facilitated & analytical
Offer to discuss
Connect delivered outputs to changes in practice and to benefits someone is accountable for.
Artefact to inspect
Benefits map, measure catalogue, dashboard and benefit-owner review notes.
Measure to agree
For each benefit, specify unit, starting value, target, observation date and owner. Show observed change and the assumed project contribution separately; record delay from output to outcome.
Scope and limits
A defined beneficiary and benefit period. Include costs and adverse effects; a dashboard does not establish attribution.

Assisted workflow orchestration

Automation option
Offer to discuss
Explore whether software assistance can draft routine task updates and route them for review.
Artefact to inspect
Workflow graph, permission and escalation rules, draft tickets or changes, and execution log.
Measure to agree
Compare median request-to-approved-action time with the existing process. Report correction rate, reviewer time and failed or duplicated actions over the same cases.
Scope and limits
One workflow and approved systems. Define human approval points and a recovery route before any external action; automation coverage alone is not value.

Digital-twin dashboard trial

Automation option
Offer to discuss
Test whether a model linked to observations gives a useful warning or a better decision.
Artefact to inspect
Model with stated boundaries, timestamped data feed, anomaly log and simulation snapshots.
Measure to agree
Mean absolute error: average absolute difference between prediction and observation, in a stated unit and horizon. Also measure alert-to-action time, missed events and false alarms.
Scope and limits
One observable process, update interval and decision. Label stale or missing data. A visual dashboard is not evidence that a simulation matches the real system.

Generative scenario lab

Automation option
Offer to discuss
Use drafted alternatives to widen a planning conversation, then test them against explicit constraints.
Artefact to inspect
Scenario set, constraint checks, risk–utility comparison and narrative briefs.
Measure to agree
Count materially different feasible alternatives after independent review. Record violated constraints and review effort; compare with alternatives produced by the existing planning method.
Scope and limits
A bounded decision and agreed utility criteria. Generated scores are proposals for scrutiny; this guide calculates neither return on investment nor confidence in it.

Assisted backlog review

Automation option
Offer to discuss
Explore clustering and draft priorities to help an owner review accumulated work.
Artefact to inspect
Cluster report, candidate work list, proposed goals and rationale log.
Measure to agree
Staleness: items without an owner review within a locally agreed age divided by items in scope. Track accepted suggestions, important omissions and total review time.
Scope and limits
One backlog with explicit priority rules. The owner approves priorities; point estimates and cluster similarity do not prove alignment with a goal.

Knowledge-graph commons

Automation option
Offer to discuss
Explore a shared map of project entities and relationships, with links back to evidence.
Artefact to inspect
Graph, vocabulary or ontology, source links and repeatable query examples.
Measure to agree
Precision at five: relevant results among the first five, judged against a fixed query set and relevance rule. Pair this with time to find a supported answer and the rate of broken evidence links.
Scope and limits
An agreed corpus, access rules and vocabulary owner. Validate extracted relationships; graph connectivity and semantic similarity do not establish truth.

Assisted compliance review

Automation option
Offer to discuss
Explore how change detection and draft comparisons can support an accountable review of requirements.
Artefact to inspect
Source-linked change report, suggested policy or clause edits, review decisions and audit trail.
Measure to agree
On a reviewed sample, count missed relevant changes and false flags; measure elapsed time from a confirmed change to an approved response. State what the sample cannot cover.
Scope and limits
Named requirements, authoritative versions and a qualified reviewer. Suggested text is not a legal or conformity conclusion; approval remains explicit.

Project conversation review

Automation option
Offer to discuss
Use meeting notes or voluntary feedback to surface topics a facilitator should check with the team.
Artefact to inspect
Source-linked topic summary, discussion map and agreed follow-up questions.
Measure to agree
Ask participants whether summaries are accurate and useful. Record corrections and follow-through on agreed actions, with a stated response rate; do not infer a morale score.
Scope and limits
A consented set of material and an agreed retention boundary. Invite people to correct interpretations; language alone does not establish wellbeing or intervention efficacy.

Predictive resource-levelling trial

Automation option
Offer to discuss
Compare a proposed forecast and allocation method with a transparent planning baseline.
Artefact to inspect
Bottleneck forecast, candidate roster, skill and availability map, and constraint checks.
Measure to agree
Over-allocation hours per resource and period; feasible assignments divided by proposed assignments. Record service shortfalls and schedule effects alongside forecast error.
Scope and limits
A defined resource pool and planning horizon, with skills, availability and labour constraints. A planner approves changes; no automatic reassignment is implied.

Option to explore

Choose the right conversation

This guide helps shape a bounded offer and its test. Service Trident starts with a perceived need and organisational level. Portfolio-office capability review examines what an organisation has now and needs later. Service options here are not individual capability assessment rows.

Nothing is uploaded or saved. Search and expansion last only for this visit. Use the browser’s link address for a named question or service; every item has a stable anchor.

Prepare a contribution to a board decision

Start with the decision or recommendation. Give just enough context to show why it matters now, then explain the current state, the financial and operational implications, and the next steps with owners and dates.

  • For a measure, show its source and date, trend, target, variance, drivers and any proposed corrective action. Distinguish checked data from unresolved assumptions.
  • Frame risk through its impact, likelihood and time horizon; show mitigations, owners and review dates. Identify what needs the board’s decision.
  • Explain cost, savings or revenue implications where supported, together with resource needs and when benefits might appear. Label estimates.
  • Keep the pre-read concise and readable; define acronyms, make comparison graphics self-explanatory, and prepare for likely questions.

Before the meeting, check that the ask is explicit, sources are current enough for it, assumptions are visible, and responsibilities and timing are clear. Three to five summary bullets may help, but completeness matters more than a fixed count.
