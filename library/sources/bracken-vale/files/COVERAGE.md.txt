# Essai 01 coverage

All 32 use cases have a substantive first version in one flat collection. This completes Essai 01 under Lawrence’s stated criterion, not every use case’s full ambition or its empirical validation. No live model, authenticated collaboration or closed-browser notification service is connected.

| ID | First version | Current mode | Wider ambition or limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | [Which account applies?](apps/constraint-evidence-librarian/README.md) | Local retrieval and register | Live evidence-grounded AI retrieval and answers over a real, maintained corpus remain open. |
| 1.2 | [Whose outcome?](apps/stakeholder-outcomes/README.md) | Local relationship model | The weights are workshop judgements. Shared stewardship and evidence of useful decisions remain untested. |
| 1.3 | [A connection has a kind](apps/relationship-layers/README.md) | Local typed network | Real evidence stewardship, attribution and usefulness remain untested; a plotted position is not influence. |
| 1.4 | [The constraint moves](apps/rail-capacity-renewal/README.md) | Local numerical model | The resource budgets and coefficients are invented. This is neither an executable timetable nor a validated investment appraisal. |
| 2.1 | [A trial worth having](apps/technology-radar/README.md) | Local trial register | The records do not authenticate participation or establish that an adoption choice is worthwhile. |
| 2.2 | [A signal worth seeing](apps/control-signal-tiles/README.md) | Local calculated signals | The signal definitions and thresholds are local assumptions; useful warning performance remains unmeasured. |
| 2.3 | [Small enough to agree](apps/micro-project-workspace/README.md) | Local workspace and recorded proposals | Live AI co-creation, authenticated simultaneous collaboration and trusted attribution remain open. |
