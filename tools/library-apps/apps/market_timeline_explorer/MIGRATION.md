## Corrections and evidence boundary

- Both retired catalogue descriptions said “ASI in 2030”. Both source bodies actually assume ASI in **Q2 2029**, and AGI in **Q4 2026**. The maintained page follows the source bodies and prominently labels these dates as assumptions. No date is a forecast, including dates which have now passed.
- The original market explorer's firm names, invented alliances, AI Act effects and corporate actions remain scenario text. They are not present corporate evidence or legal guidance. No current factual corporate, regulatory or capability claim was introduced.
- Numerical claims such as 10× productivity, 80% work removal, 70% consultancy losses, the archetype story's 1/10th time and 1/2 cost, and the original market story's 25% schedule compression remain unevidenced narrative premises. No calculation validates them.
- “AGI” does not imply deployment, adoption, market power or human benefit. ASI does not establish post-scarcity. Those links are story assumptions, visible in the source-and-limits disclosure and the causal sketch caption.
- The stories share a premise and are not independent evidence, a probability distribution, exhaustive scenario planning or an investment recommendation.
- Model labels are source catalogue provenance. Publication dates have not been invented. The migration review date is distinct from story dates and original authorship.

## Routes and validation

Forward the old Gemini page to `/library/apps/market_timeline_explorer/#gemini`; forward the old Navigator page to `/library/apps/market_timeline_explorer/#navigator`.

Canonical selected-state examples:

- `#story=market&period=2026Q4`
- `#story=gemini&period=2027Q3` (the uneven plateau)
- `#story=navigator&period=2029Q2`

Simple `#market`, `#gemini` and `#navigator` open each story's first period. Original source-name hashes are accepted as aliases. Choosing a period replaces the current hash, avoiding dozens of browser-history entries; it does not save user reflections.

Run from `tools/library-apps` using Node 24:

```sh
node node_modules/vitest/vitest.mjs run --root . apps/market_timeline_explorer/src/App.test.jsx
APP=market_timeline_explorer node node_modules/vite/bin/vite.js build
```
