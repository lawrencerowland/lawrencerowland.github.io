01 · Construct a path

Can we keep the whole promise?

Require continued service, shared crews, restricted access or an intermediate milestone. Find feasible action sequences and inspect every step behind the resource trade-offs.

How the parts fit

A choice must have an implementation.

Specify what the system must provide. Keep combinations whose parts satisfy each other’s requirements. Then compare the resources those complete implementations need.

The rail path, programme and wildlife models use this common structure at different levels. Their method notes show the actual interfaces and the limits of the construction.
