# Emblems with scenario stamps

A provisional visual study, not a replacement for the current catalogue tiles.

Open `/design/emblem-scenario-stamps/` on the site or a repository-root preview server. The HTML uses existing local SVGs without modifying or redrawing them. The experiment is the main image; the scenario sits at the upper right as an embossed paper stamp. The extra canvas above the emblem keeps the stamp away from its marks. The larger-picture control allows a closer comparison.

`pairs.json` records all 15 distinct catalogue-supported experiment/scenario pairs across 12 scenarios. Its authority is `_data/project_scenarios.json` (`project_ids` checked against the individual project entries), joined to `_data/experiment_tiles.json`. Source assets were checked to exist. Titles and links use those existing records; repeated app entries do not manufacture additional pairings.

Island Reading Room currently belongs to the Functors for Projects collection and uses that family emblem. It does not yet have a dedicated Geometry of Interaction emblem in this collection. The two reading maps, Meta Project Innovation and Counterfactual programme steering have no scenario entrance in this catalogue. The Gimmer comparison has no independent experiment emblem. They are not assigned invented pictures.

The preview is deliberately isolated under `design/`; no production tiles are changed. If the treatment is adopted later, catalogue data should generate the pairings so the preview snapshot cannot become a competing catalogue.
